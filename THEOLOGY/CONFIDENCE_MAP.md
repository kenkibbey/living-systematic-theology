<h1 align="center">Step 2 — LST Confidence Map</h1>
<p align="center"><em>An easy-to-scan guide to confession, synthesis, possibilities, and uncertainty</em></p>
<p align="center">✦ <strong>Scripture governs</strong> · Evidence matters · Every claim remains testable ✦</p>

**Current Systematic: Version 2.7 (October 21, 2026).** This is a navigational index of selected major claims, **not a replacement for the chapter's detailed wording**. Some chapters contain different claims at different confidence levels; a grade attaches to an exact proposition, not to a whole chapter.

## One confidence standard

- **Confession** — the claim is established by direct and/or compelling biblical evidence.
- **Defended synthesis** — the current conclusion is strongly argued from the whole biblical case but requires real inference.
- **Argued possibility** — a reading has positive support but is not demonstrated as required.
- **Not demonstrated / unresolved** — evidence does not currently settle the exact claim.

A foundational **historical and canonical judgment** (such as identifying the 66-book canon) has a distinct evidentiary basis; it should not be mislabeled a verse that directly supplies a table of contents.
