## Purpose

Test whether a structured four-role review improves the quality and independence of biblical adjudication without making LST's current wording automatically favored or new theories automatically accepted.

**The first two roles advocate opposite cases, not against the people participating.** Prompt A is the **Challenger's Advocate**, making the best fair biblical case for the submitter's challenge against LST. Prompt B is the disclosed LST defender. The independent adjudicator must not favor either, and the verifier must test whether the decision follows from the passages. All share the same grammatical, historical, literary and canonical controls.

## Required tasks for each trial

1. **Freeze the case.** Identify exact LST version and disputed words, original public challenge (including rebuttals), complete defender response, proposed correction, source-access limitations, and a citation to the issue's exact GitHub state. Do not rely on a single partisan summary.
2. **Record assignments.** Example: Grok = challenger's advocate; a separately instructed AI = LST defender (Prompt B); Claude = independent adjudicator; Gemini = independent verifier. All four provider assignments may vary. Separate the advocate, defender, judge, and verifier conversations, and document any actual additional context instead of presuming the new defender uses T.A.I.T. instructions. For historical Issue #33, retain its original project-assisted B attribution.
3. **Record public role prompts and actual context.** Current reusable prompts: [A — Challenger's Advocate](PROMPT_A_CHALLENGE_ADVOCATE.md) (historical reports may name [the earlier A examiner prompt](PROMPT_A_ADVERSARIAL_EXAMINER.md)), [B](PROMPT_B_LST_DEFENDER.md), [C](PROMPT_C_INDEPENDENT_ADJUDICATOR.md), [D](PROMPT_D_INDEPENDENT_VERIFIER.md). For every run record its real prompt/version, provider/model (if known), date, source packet, memory/customization limits, and additional instructions **if any**. Do not invent private T.A.I.T. instructions in a standalone B run; do not erase those instructions from the historical #33 report.
4. **Do not decide from prose alone.** Each reviewer must access the passages used as actual evidence, disclose source limitations, and distinguish the challenge's merit from the current doctrine's affirmative biblical warrant.
5. **Test position-order effects.** When practical, blind A/B party labels for the initial adjudication and perform a fresh swapped-order rerun. Record any changed conclusions.
6. **Publish the disagreement.** The verifier must identify material problems, not invent agreement. A major discrepancy means adjudication is not verified; a second visible examination may be necessary.
7. **Evaluate the method itself.** Look for failure to concede a genuine LST error, failure to reject a poorly supported replacement, poor source work, unsupported lexical claims, excessive hedging, and inflation of doctrinal confidence.

## Public archive and original-issue comment routing

**One substantive issue per challenge. Do not open a companion issue for full AI examinations.** The public GitHub issue remains the continuing topic record for submissions, status notices, official adjudication, and relevant contributions from any participant. Substantive issues remain open and unlocked by default, without a fixed rebuttal-round limit or response deadline. For each admitted review create a folder `REVIEW/CASES/ISSUE-XXXX/` (padded issue number), with `README.md` and **complete, separately attributed Markdown files** for Prompt A, B, C and D reports as each is verified. The current example is [Issue #33's case archive](CASES/ISSUE-0033/README.md).

**Maintain the current summary:** Record the present LST position and version, verdict or review status, main evidence and reasoning, verified changes, and unresolved questions, linking adjudications and revisions. Keep that summary distinct from the preserved history of earlier arguments and decisions and from GitHub open/closed state. A completed adjudication does not end contributions. Human assessment, Revision Resistance for material evidence or errors, and owner approval for substantive changes apply; no immediate response or repeated full adjudication of repetitive comments is guaranteed.

**Related issues, administrative exceptions, and moderation:** Link related issues; genuinely distinct challenges may have their own linked issue. Before closing a duplicate, inspect and preserve distinct evidence or argument in the continuing issue or a linked record, then redirect with the canonical link and reason. Misplaced matters may be redirected with a link or explanation, and explicit requests to close resolved or withdrawn cases may be honored. Acceptance alone does not close the topic. Administrative closure and locking require separate recorded reasons; theological disagreement does not justify locking. Apply the [continuing review and moderation rules](../CONTRIBUTING.md#continuing-review-and-issue-status). Previously closed cases retain their recorded history, with reopening a separate maintainer decision. Participation counts prove no doctrine.

**First substantive AI response:** After human intake and after the Prompt A **Challenger's Advocate** report is actually completed and archived, post a **short, respectful first substantive AI response** under the original public challenge, describing the assigned advocate's task as presenting the issue in its best biblical form against LST and linking to the **verified complete report**. Administrative intake comments may precede it; do not make a false chronological claim. Subsequent B/C/D outputs each receive a brief notice and a verified link in the **same original issue**.

**Preserve provenance:** Full reports and their prompt IDs/model information remain immutable in substance; publish later corrections as separately attributed amendments or version-controlled changes with visible explanations. Earlier A runs under **Adversarial Examiner** remain historically labeled, even though the current proposed role is **Challenger's Advocate**. The case archive does not establish a new issue, peer-reviewed human finding, doctrinal verdict or new official policy. Do not report an unrun role as complete.

**Publishing a read-only AI's results:** The owner reports Gemini can now read relevant Google Drive and GitHub sources, but cannot post to GitHub. Once Gemini produces its **complete original report**, the owner may transfer it to an authorized Project Engineer publishing session. Record **Gemini** as report author/examiner and **ChatGPT / Project Engineer** solely as publishing intermediary; preserve Gemini's substantive wording and all stated source limits, report version/date, and available provenance. Do not convert a Gemini access claim into verified source checking without the actual report's evidence. Publish a separately attributed complete Markdown report under the existing case folder, read it back, then link it in a brief notice on the **same original GitHub issue** when authorized. Never create a companion issue or mislabel the publisher as the examiner.

## What a report must contain

- Exact public prompt version or commit SHA, full input list, actual model assignments, and what each model truly had available.
- Original challenger material and LST defender's independent strongest scriptural case.
- Adjudicator's position-by-position, passage-by-passage analysis and distinct ratings for **challenge outcome** and **positive doctrinal warrant**.
- Verifier's truly independent critique, supporting and opposing passages checked, omissions, bias checks, and disagreement.
- Whether a proposal to change LST was made, and the difference between a *recommendation*, *approved change*, *verified Drive edit*, and *verified GitHub public release*.
