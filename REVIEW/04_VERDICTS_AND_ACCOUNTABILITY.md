<h1 align="center">4. Verdicts, Corrections, and Public Accountability</h1>
<p align="center"><em>Evidence-based decisions and a traceable public record</em></p>
<p align="center">✦ <strong>Scripture governs</strong> · Evidence matters · Every claim remains testable ✦</p>

## The judgment must be based on Scripture

The reviewer must determine whether (a) a challenge demonstrates a fault, (b) the current LST doctrine has *positive biblical warrant at its claimed confidence*, and (c) the challenger’s replacement is itself warranted. These are three different questions. Failure of the attack is not proof of the defense; a valid criticism is not automatic proof of a replacement.

Possible dispositions are **SUSTAINED, PARTIALLY SUSTAINED, NOT SUSTAINED, or UNRESOLVED**. Appropriate recommendations can include retain, strengthen arguments, qualify language, adjust confidence, revise, abandon, or identify a question for further examination. No theory is added just because it sounds plausible; contradictions with other teachings require further Scripture-based testing.

Substantive challenge issues remain open and unlocked by default under the [continuing review rules](../CONTRIBUTING.md#continuing-review-and-issue-status). A maintained summary identifies the present position and version, verdict or review status, evidence considered, verified changes, and unresolved questions separately from GitHub open/closed state. Any contributor may add relevant evidence, arguments, or corrections without a fixed reply limit or response deadline. The maintainer or an authorized human reviewer assesses those contributions; a material error or previously overlooked evidence can warrant renewed Revision Resistance. Participation counts and issue state establish no theological conclusion.

## Required public evidence

Every actual review should record the issue, quoted claim and version, challenger’s original biblical argument, defender’s strongest biblical case, independently verified controlling passages, the independent judge’s finding, and the verifier’s substantive objections or concurrence. Each AI role must record its provider/model if known, prompt version, input materials, date, and the evidence it could not independently check.

The report should separate **what the criticism demonstrated** from **what the current LST claim positively proved**. It should also show any surviving objection, source dispute, recommended change, and impact on other LST doctrines. Provide the reasoned finding, not a numerical vote of four AI systems.

The record must not claim that any specific AI ran until its actual run has been verified. If additional private instructions or unavailable source material materially influenced a run, disclose that limitation and do not label the run “fully prompt-transparent.”

## What happens if the two independent models disagree?

The judge’s reasoning is examined against the verifier’s specific objections. A real error should result in a new, visibly documented assessment—not an averaged compromise or a quiet change in result. Some issues will remain unresolved.

Changing the order of the two positions and checking whether the finding changes is useful when feasible, though it cannot eliminate all model bias.

## Who controls publication?

The human repository owner still manages access, sensitive data, version history, and authorized publication. This is **not a claim that humans have literally disappeared from the process**. The intended theological assessment comes from an independent, openly checked review, rather than a private owner opinion. A disagreement by the owner should be disclosed as such and subject to further biblical examination; it cannot be disguised as the independent judge’s finding.

An AI recommendation is not a completed file change. If approved, a governing change must first preserve the prior state in Old Systematics, update the current Drive document, update the Revision Record where warranted, verify the Drive state, and independently publish and verify corresponding GitHub changes.

## This is a public pilot design

The current official process remains the one published in the existing GitHub challenge rules until the pilot is tested, a methodology revision is examined under Revision Resistance, the repository owner authorizes the change, the governing Drive materials are archived/updated, and the GitHub policy is synchronized and verified. Prior cases do not become independent four-model verdicts retroactively.
