<h1 align="center">Step 4 — Who Runs LST and How AI Is Used</h1>
<p align="center"><em>A Scripture-governed reading path: clear, careful, and open to examination</em></p>
<p align="center">✦ <strong>Scripture governs</strong> · Evidence matters · Every claim remains testable ✦</p>

Living Systematic Theology is maintained by **Ken Kibbey** (GitHub account: **kenkibbey**). He is responsible for the repository, access, privacy, historical preservation, official versions, and authorized publication.

**T.A.I.T. | The AI Theologian** is a ChatGPT-based theological research and discussion assistant informed by the current LST documents. It can study and compare biblical passages, trace arguments, and help construct the strongest legitimate case for retaining LST. It is not an independent theological referee.

The distinction matters. A defender intentionally argues for an existing position. A neutral adjudicator should not be told to favor that position. Neither the defender nor an independent judge has the authority of Scripture.

## The proposed independent arrangement

A public **four-AI review pilot** has been documented but **has not yet been adopted as the operative challenge procedure**. The four roles are challenger-side advocate, LST defender, independent adjudicator, and independent verifier. **Any AI provider may perform a role** in its own conversation when it has sufficient evidence; the owner currently uses a separate **LST Defender Revision Resistance** project for the defense rather than T.A.I.T.'s private project instructions. No commercial AI can be proved perfectly unbiased.

**All four proposed task prompts are publicly readable** in Step 6. The standalone defender receives its instructions from the published [Prompt B](../REVIEW/PROMPT_B_LST_DEFENDER.md), according to the owner. Examine each report's actual input and source-access record, rather than presuming hidden T.A.I.T. instructions or guaranteeing that any AI is fully isolated. The original Issue #33 ChatGPT defense used the earlier project-assisted arrangement; that historical attribution remains unchanged.

A proposed independent AI finding is still fallible. Human responsibility for permission, archival, and publication does not disappear. The owner should not present a private preference as if it were an independent AI verdict. The Bible remains the judge of theological truth.
