# LST Plain-Language Glossary

*Short explanations of words you may encounter while reading Living Systematic Theology*

This is a **reader's aid**, not a replacement for the [Creed](LST_CREED.md) or [full Systematic](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md). Some words have different meanings across Christian traditions. The linked sections show what LST actually argues.

**These explanations describe LST's current language and positions, not unchangeable conclusions.** If Scripture requires LST to revise a claim, the current Systematic—and, where needed, this guide—should change to match.

## Understanding the method

**Systematic theology:** Studying the Bible's teaching on related subjects together, rather than treating each passage as isolated. See [LST's foundation](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#1-theological-foundation-authority-and-living-method).

**Canon:** The collection of books accepted as Scripture. LST receives the 66 books of the Old and New Testaments as its authoritative written Scripture. See [Scripture](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#2-scripture-and-its-interpretation).

**Exegesis:** Carefully examining what a biblical passage says in its own setting, language, and argument.

**Hermeneutics:** Principles used to interpret a text. For LST, context and the biblical author's meaning govern conclusions. See [Scripture and interpretation](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#2-scripture-and-its-interpretation).

**Confession / what we confess:** A doctrine LST believes Scripture establishes at its stated confidence level. It does not mean that LST is incapable of correction.

**Defended synthesis:** A theological conclusion LST considers supported by multiple biblical passages and careful reasoning, but not stated in full as a single direct biblical proposition.

**Unresolved:** A question on which LST does not claim that the available evidence proves one answer.

**Argued possibility:** An interpretation with a positive biblical case, but without enough evidence to call it demonstrated. It is not automatically LST's settled belief.

**Counterevidence / countertext:** A biblical passage or argument that appears to challenge a proposed interpretation and must be taken seriously.

**Revision Resistance:** LST's practice of building the strongest biblical case for retaining its present claim **before** deciding whether to revise it, and then testing both sides by the same standard. See the [public procedure](LST_REVISION_RESISTANCE_GUIDE.md).

## Understanding theological terms

**Trinity:** One God who is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—three distinct, coeternal persons equal in deity. See [the Triune God](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#3-the-triune-god).

**Eternal generation:** A theological way of describing the Son's eternal relation to the Father; it does **not** mean Jesus was created or began to exist. LST defends this as a synthesis. See [the Triune God](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#3-the-triune-god).

**Divine decree:** God's eternal purpose concerning what comes to pass. LST distinguishes the biblical unity of that purpose from the technical claim that it consists of numerically one decree. See [decree and providence](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#4-the-eternal-decree-and-providence).

**Providence:** God's ongoing rule over and care for creation, including real human actions and responsibility. See [decree and providence](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#4-the-eternal-decree-and-providence).

**Sovereign Actualization:** LST's name for the way God's eternal purpose is certainly accomplished in real history through ordained ends and means. It is a significant synthesis, **not** an extra rule that overrides Scripture. See [LST's foundation](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#1-theological-foundation-authority-and-living-method).

**Covenant:** A binding relationship or arrangement established by God and revealed through His promises and dealings with people. See [the covenants](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#12-the-covenants-and-the-unity-of-gods-purpose).

**Atonement:** Christ's saving work in dealing with sin and reconciling people to God. See [the atonement](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#7-the-atonement-accomplishment-extent-and-proclamation).

**Election:** God's choosing, especially as Scripture speaks of His saving purpose. See [salvation by sovereign grace](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#9-salvation-by-sovereign-grace).

**Regeneration:** God's work of giving spiritual life—often called being born again. See [salvation by sovereign grace](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#9-salvation-by-sovereign-grace).

**Justification:** God's declaring a sinner righteous through Christ rather than on the basis of the sinner's own merit. See [salvation by sovereign grace](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#9-salvation-by-sovereign-grace).

**Sanctification:** The work of making believers holy in their lives and conduct. See [Christian obedience](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#19-law-gospel-and-christian-obedience).

**Perseverance:** Continuing in faith in Christ. LST defends God's preservation of His people **through** persevering faith, repentance, and obedience, while taking biblical warnings seriously. See [preservation and perseverance](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#11-preservation-perseverance-and-assurance).

**Ordinances:** Practices Christ gave the Church, especially baptism and the Lord's Supper. See [the Church and its ordinances](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#20-the-church-its-mission-and-its-ordinances).

**Eschatology:** Study of what Scripture teaches about Christ's return, resurrection, judgment, and the final future. See [prophecy and the millennium](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#17-prophecy-the-millennium-and-the-gathering-of-the-saints).

**Millennium:** The thousand years mentioned in Revelation 20. LST does not claim to have settled every question about the nature and placement of this period. See [prophecy and the millennium](LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md#17-prophecy-the-millennium-and-the-gathering-of-the-saints).
