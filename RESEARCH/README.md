# Comparative Research and Methodology Notes

Research in this folder **informs** Living Systematic Theology; it **does not govern doctrine or method**. The current [Systematic](../LIVING_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.md) and the applicable governing LST documents take precedence.

## Read the comparison

**[How Distinctive Is Living Systematic Theology? — A Source-Checked Comparison of Revisable Christian Theology](COMPARATIVE_STUDY_OF_REVISABLE_THEOLOGY_2026-10-21.md)**  
October 21, 2026 · Current LST baseline: Version 2.9 / Revision 50

This study compares LST's actual revision workflow with eight historical, denominational, and digital research projects. It identifies real precedents for open, revisable theology and evaluates whether any documented example combines LST's systematic scope, confidence categories, structured Revision Resistance, public dispute history, archived superseded states and semantic versioning. It includes a comparison table, links to primary sources, a candid account of the initial AI research draft's errors, and limits on any novelty claim.

**Main finding:** LST appears distinctive **among the documented comparisons**, especially in how it combines several established practices. No exhaustive search has demonstrated that LST is the only such theological undertaking. The four-AI review is a **public pilot**, not an adopted official challenge procedure.

### Feedback

Readers who can identify a closer analogue are invited to share verified primary sources and actual revision examples. A substantiated comparison should improve this non-governing study rather than be dismissed.
