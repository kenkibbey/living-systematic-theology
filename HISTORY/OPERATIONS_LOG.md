## Accuracy and privacy

Public adjudications should explain Scripture, sources, unresolved objections, and actual review provenance without demanding trust in internal conversations. Internal operating instructions, private correspondence and participant histories are not themselves published as evidence; this protects personal information while placing the public **rules** and **reasoned outcomes** under scrutiny.

For the exact doctrinal rationale of Version 2.7, consult the [Revision Record](../LST_REVISION_RECORD.md). Administrative improvements documented here have no independent authority to revise doctrine.
