<h1 align="center">Step 1 — LST Doctrinal Change Overview</h1>
<p align="center"><em>A brief record of important theological developments</em></p>
<p align="center">✦ <strong>Scripture governs</strong> · Evidence matters · Every claim remains testable ✦</p>

A short public orientation to significant theological versions. The [complete Revision Record](../LST_REVISION_RECORD.md) preserves individual arguments and dates; this overview must not be mistaken for the full history.

| Version | Major development |
| --- | --- |
| **2.7 — October 21, 2026** | Clarified neutral descriptions of Revelation 20, the unresolved millennial options, ethnic Israel's future saving mercy, and the Creed's mixed confidence categories; preserved existing doctrines while correcting wording and Scripture references |
| **2.6 — October 10, 2026** | Calibrated confidence in the numerical-singleness-of-the-decree synthesis, updated certain public interpretive controls, and documented independent-audit limitations |
| **2.5 — October 2026** | Clarified eternal Father–Son relation, mediatorial order, His unending reign, and His final subjection |
| **2.4 — October 2026** | Clarified equality in divine essence alongside real personal and mediatorial order |
| **2.1–2.2 — October 2026** | Refined the millennium, creation-day duration, reckoned righteousness, and disputed interpretations without treating every uncertainty as a doctrinal reversal |
| **2.0 — October 2026** | Withdrew the Western Filioque formula from confession-level status when its exact technical proposition could not be established from Scripture |
| **1.x — October 2026** | Built the initial system and subjected its doctrinal scope, wording, confidence levels, and revision discipline to repeated biblical testing |

Only the **current Systematic** has the active theological version; this summary records where the project has been. Theological releases should occur when the biblical evidence warrants material refinement, not for every typo or website change.

When a claim is under examination, consult the governing wording that was actually in effect during that issue. Git commit history records public changes; historical Drive snapshots preserve governing states. Future version tags and release notes will make this lookup easier, but do not presume that old tags or PDFs exist until verified.
